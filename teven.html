MY BLACK LADY

QUEEN DEI REALNARDI

Queen Dei Realnardi is our fully black German Shepherd , and we look forward to seeing her grow and develop.

At just 10 months old, Queen is a young German Shepherd with a striking, fully black coat and a pedigree rooted in working lines. She is a special part of our family and of the journey we are building with Haus Esposito Kennel.

HER PEDIGREE

Working-Line Heritage

Queen's ancestry includes established German Shepherd bloodlines, with names such as Eros vom Ihorster Busch, Xsiva del Lupo Nero, Wolf von Prevent and Dr onko vom Alten Försterhaus.

HER JOURNEY

Growing & Developing

Queen is still young, and her journey is only beginning. For us, the most important thing is to give her the time, care and training she needs to grow and develop at her own pace.
