OUR MALE

WITO Cevaro

WITO Cevaro represents our passion for the working-line German Shepherd, a breed we admire for its character, intelligence, working ability and loyalty.

ACHIEVEMENTS

Recognition & Körung

At a breed show, WITO was awarded Vorzüglich (V) by Judge Weber, recognising his excellent qualities according to the breed standard.

On 10 October 2026, WITO successfully passed his Körung in Traunstein, Bavaria, Germany, marking an important milestone in his development.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Passion & Responsibility

At Haus Esposito Kennel, our passion for the German Shepherd goes beyond appearance and titles. We value sound character, working ability and respect for the breed's heritage.

We believe in learning, making thoughtful decisions and approaching breeding with honesty and responsibility. We are proud of WITO and look forward to continuing our journey with him, guided by our genuine passion for the breed and respect for what makes the working-line German Shepherd so exceptional.

BREEDING

Stud Service

WITO Cevaro is available for stud service. We welcome enquiries from owners of suitable females who share our passion for the working-line German Shepherd and our commitment to responsible breeding.

For breeding enquiries and further information, please contact Haus Esposito Kennel.
